# Portfolio-Ready Standard

Work is `portfolio-ready` only when:

- the assignment is complete and the tutor has approved it;
- no substantive review comments remain unresolved;
- setup, process, decisions, and result are understandable to an external viewer;
- tests, reproducibility steps, screenshots, previews, or exports are included where appropriate;
- the student's contribution is clearly identifiable and collaborators are credited;
- secrets, personal information, confidential briefs, restricted datasets, and unlicensed assets are removed;
- links work and the public presentation is professional;
- publication is permitted by the assignment, data license, asset license, and any client agreement.

## Referencing work

The actual work remains in the course repository, capstone repository, or an explicitly authorized personal repository. `student-projects` stores an approved catalog entry linking to the work, its merged pull request or case study, and a short description. It must not duplicate every student's source files.

Students may link approved public work from a profile README, portfolio website, or personal case-study repository. Forking or copying is optional and must respect licenses, collaborators, and tutor guidance. The academy never modifies a student's personal repository without explicit authorization.
